# Raider CivicsLab

The math of the common good: budgets as moral documents, small choices that add up to big patterns, and public claims tested against official numbers. Built for Regis Jesuit Theology and social studies, organized by Catholic social teaching and what the U.S. bishops have asked of public life.

**Live site:** https://rjedtech.github.io/Raider-CivicsLab/ (not linked from the other Raider Labs sites)

## House rules
- Tools name programs, laws and documents, never political parties.
- Each side's strongest case is shown; the bishops' positions are quoted with sources, and Church teaching is distinguished from prudential judgment.
- Every claim is tested the same way, including claims in the bishops' own letters.
- Math questions have right answers; value questions do not, and no tool scores one.
- Figures carry the date they were checked. Pending court cases are marked as alleged.
